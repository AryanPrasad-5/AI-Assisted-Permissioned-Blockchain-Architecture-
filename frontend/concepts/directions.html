Three design directions

Each direction is drawn on the same three screens with real numbers from the M2, X1 and Phase 1C records. Rendered at 1280×800, shown at 42%. The miniature charts are composition sketches only; the prototype draws them from data.

A. Console: enterprise research intelligence

Graphite dark UI, one cool accent, sidebar workspace, KPI tiles, dense panels. Plex Sans at tight tracking. Strong for a tool people use daily; weak at telling a story with a null result, because the KPI-tile pattern makes four equal numbers look like a scoreboard.

B. Lab report: scientific research laboratory

Cool report paper, slate ink, one ultramarine for interaction. Condensed Plex headlines, Plex Serif for reading. Every figure carries a caption and a provenance margin (source file, evidence class). The hero is the data itself: the ledger of all 120 M2 decisions, where every compared cell reads B = 2.

CutpointResearchExperimentsPaperSource

Three controllers made 90 decisions. Every one chose B = 2.

We compared static, reactive and predictive block-size control live on Hyperledger Fabric. They tied, and the constant-rate scans show why: there was no decision to make.
